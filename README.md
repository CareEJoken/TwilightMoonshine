# Twilight Moonshine

*中文说明见 [README_zh.md](README_zh.md)*

**Twilight Moonshine** is a Twaddon made with [NeoForge](https://neoforged.net/) and dedicated to **Twilight Forest** lore. Visit the twilight lake, befriend Moon Rabbits, mix potions and watch the forest's little creatures play music in the moonlight.

Requires Minecraft **1.21.1**, NeoForge **21.1.x** and **Twilight Forest 4.8+**.

## Features

### Moon Rabbits & the Moon
- **Moon Rabbit** — a new rabbit friends found near the Twilight Lake. Feed them carrots to make them grow bigger!
- **Moonlight Moonshine** — a drink that grants Fire Resistance, Water Breathing and Night Vision for 8 minutes. Hand the Moon Rabbit three distinct potions to receive one.
- **Moon Stone** — a new stone family: shards, full blocks, bricks, stairs, slabs, walls and shard piles.
- *Moon Bell & the "Full Moon" advancement — work in progress, not included in this release.*

### Brewing & Alchemy
- New potion effects: **Resistance**, **Faintly Glowing**, **Glow** and **Twilight**, each available in regular / extended forms, splash & lingering variants, tipped arrows and spectral arrows.
- **Secret recipes** — discover the hidden recipes for Twilight Plant Extract, Twilight Glow Essence and Twilight Alloy Powder through Secret Pages.
- **Twilight Alloy Powder** & **Secret Pages** — discover secret recipes and craft them up.

### Twilight Forest Adjustments (server-side datapack + mixins)
- **Mushroom Tower & Quest Island** re-added to world generation, with a custom map decorative.
- Twilight light: a custom dusk light curve and a tinted light map for the Twilight Forest dimension.
- New advancements: *Stomp Stomp!*, *Embrace Day*, **Sunset Beetle Band**, *River of Light*, *Twilight Sentinel*, *From Hand to Hand* and *The Coolidge Family*.

### Sunset Beetle Band (Sunset Beetle Trio)
Gather a Slime Beetle, a Fire Beetle and a Pinch Beetle near a jukebox, put a record on and the three beetles will turn to face the music, hop to the beat, and play a little **three-part round**: bass, bell and pling, each beetle with its own colour notes.

## Requirements

| Dependency | Version |
|------------|---------|
| Minecraft | 1.21.1 |
| NeoForge | 21.1.x |
| Twilight Forest | 4.8+ |

The Twilight Forest jar is **required** (a plain jar is allowed — no sources shipped). This mod is installed client-side; most features work on servers, dedicated server installations also work.

## Installation

1. Install **NeoForge 21.1.x** (21.1.191+ recommended) for Minecraft 1.21.1.
2. Install **Twilight Forest** for 1.21.1 in the same `mods` folder.
3. Drop `twilightmoonshine-1.0.0.jar` (and `twilightforest-*universal.jar`) into `mods`.
4. Launch. A new **Twilight Moonshine** creative tab is available.
