# Twilight Moonshine Changelog

## 1.0.0

Initial release. Requires Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.x, and Twilight Forest 4.8+.

### Moon Rabbits & the Moon

- **Moon rabbits**: new friendly twilight mob with their own sounds (ambient/hurt/death/jump), a sneeze mechanic that scatters items, and a grow-on-feeding feature that powers the "Stomp Stomp!" advancement.
- **Moon rabbit trophy** (block + wall variant): a display trophy for your base.
- **Moonshine** potion: drinking grants 8 minutes of Fire Resistance + Water Breathing + Night Vision. Traded by moon rabbits in exchange for three distinct potions (Twilight, Glowing, Resistance).

### Brewing & Alchemy

- New potions: **Resistance** (3 tiers, brewing recipe removed), **Glowing** (base + long), and **Twilight** (the music-birthing embrace effect).
- **Twilight Embrace** effect: while active, twilight forest animals gather to you and hostile creatures turn friendly.
- **Secret page** system: interactive pages that unlock one of several hidden recipes (moon rabbit sneezes / luring animals / glowing trails are the item's real-world acquisition channels).
- New craftable items: **twilight plant extract**, **twilight glow essence**, **twilight alloy powder**, **moon stone shards** → **moon stone bricks** → **moon stone building family** (block, stairs, slab, wall).
- Potion-arrow crafting: turning a potion + 8 arrows into 8 effect arrows via a dedicated recipe serializer.

### Twilight Forest Adjustments

- **Mushroom Tower** and **Quest Island** re-enabled in generation (Twilight Forest still has these commented out), including a custom map decoration for the tower so it shows on the magic map / in locate results.
- Rebalanced twilight daylight color curve.
- 7 new advancements including "Embrace Day" (10 passive mobs around you with Twilight Embrace), "The Sunset Beetle Band" (three beetle species + a jukebox nearby), and a glowing-boss reward.

### Sunset Beetle Band

- The sunset beetle advancement: gather Slime Beetle + Fire Beetle + Pinch Beetle around a jukebox to unlock it.

### Notes

- The Moon Bell is still work-in-progress and is not included in this release.
- This mod embeds no Twilight Forest code; it is a NeoForge mod loading against the Twilight Forest jar.
